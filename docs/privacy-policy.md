# Focus privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Focus is a focus timer for Android. This policy describes what the app does with your data.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter (your goal, tasks, plans, sessions, notes, and the list of allowed apps) is stored in a database on your phone. Focus has no servers and no accounts. It makes no network calls, so none of this data leaves the device through the app.

- No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising.
- No data is collected, sold, or shared with anyone.

## Android backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android includes the Focus database in your device backup, the same way it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by Focus. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Export

Export data writes one file with your data and opens the Android share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

## Permissions

Each permission is used only during a session you start, and only on the device:

- **Do Not Disturb access**: silences notifications during a session and restores your previous setting afterwards. Calls still ring.
- **Usage access**: checks which app is in the foreground, so apps that are not on your allowed list can be blocked. Usage data is read on the device and never stored or sent.
- **Display over other apps**: brings up the block screen when a blocked app opens.
- **Phone state**: detects when a call starts and ends, so call time is not counted against a session. Phone numbers are not read.
- **Notifications**: shows the ongoing session notification and optional start-time reminders.
- **Ignore battery optimization**: keeps a session running while the screen is off.
